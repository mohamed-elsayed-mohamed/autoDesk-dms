# Spec prompt: 002 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

**Use with:** `/speckit.specify` — paste the content below into the command.

---

Build AutoDesk DMS — a Dealer Management System for automotive dealerships. This feature is **002-crm**: Customer Relationship Management so that every interaction with customers and leads is tracked and no lead falls through the cracks.

**Why:** Customer information is often scattered across notebooks and inboxes. Sales and BDC staff need a 360-degree view of each customer and lead, with a clear pipeline (New → Contacted → Appointment Set → Showed → Negotiating → Sold/Lost) and follow-up tasks. The CRM ensures consistent follow-up and gives management visibility into lead sources and conversion.

**Prerequisites:** This feature assumes 001-vehicle-inventory (and auth) exists. Customers may reference vehicles of interest from inventory.

**User Story 1 (P1) — Customer profiles**  
As a Sales Consultant or BDC agent I can create and edit customer profiles with name, phone, email, address, preferred contact method, and optional notes so that we have one record per person. I can search customers by name, phone, or email. The system warns me when I might be creating a duplicate (same phone or email). I can view a customer's full profile and see linked leads and activities.

**User Story 2 (P2) — Lead capture and pipeline**  
As a BDC agent or Sales Consultant I can create a lead and link it to a customer (or create the customer at the same time). I must record the lead source (Website, Phone, WalkIn, AutoTrader, Cars.com, or Other). I can assign the lead to a salesperson (manual or round-robin). Each lead has a status: New, Contacted, Appointment Set, Showed, Negotiating, Sold, or Lost. I can move the lead through the pipeline and optionally link a vehicle of interest from inventory. I can list leads filtered by status, assignee, source, and date range.

**User Story 3 (P3) — Activities and timeline**  
As any staff member I can log an activity on a lead or customer: type (call, email, text, visit, note), direction (inbound/outbound), optional content, and it is stamped with who did it and when. I can view a chronological timeline of all activities for a customer across all their leads so we have a full history of touchpoints.

**User Story 4 (P4) — Follow-up tasks**  
As a Sales Consultant I can create a follow-up task linked to a lead (e.g. "Call back Tuesday 2pm", "Send quote by Friday") with a due date and optional description. I can see "my tasks for today" in one place and mark tasks complete or cancel them. Overdue tasks are clearly indicated.

**Out of scope for this feature:** Deals, F&I, service, parts, accounting, OEM integration, AI lead scoring, and email/SMS sending (logging only). Use correct domain language (lead, BDC, up, be-back); see dealer-management-system-guide.md for definitions.
